[Eleanor Roosevelt] once said:
  "Great minds discuss ideas;
  average minds discuss events;
  small minds discuss people."
I want to swap the first and the second and illustrate by example.
This is the list of questions I ask on a first date.
The principle may be applied to any meetings, either romantic or professional.
What matters is the depth of discussions.
The deeper, the better.
Also---the riskier.

<!--more-->

{% jb_picture_body %}

**You vs Reality**

At the level you talk about facts that surround you both:

* How old are you?
* Do you like chocolate?
* Do you have a pet?
* Where do you work?

Even an idiot can answer these questions, accurately.
You basically exchange information that doesn't attack anyone's beliefs.
Even if you don't like chocolate and I do, it doesn't make me any lesser.
If you have a cat and I hate cats, I'm not insulted by your choice.
We are safe, since we provide no interpretation of the reality around us.
We just exchange factual information.
Safe and boring.

**You vs Events**

Then, you talk about situations and your attitude to them:

* What do you do to stay fit?
* Do you know how chocolate is made?
* How do you train your dog?
* How did you get your current job?

**You vs People**

Next level, you can talk about the behavior of others or your own behavior:

* What was your grandma like?
*
* How do you know that your cat loves you?
* Why your boss doesn't give you a raise?



**You vs Ideas**

Finally, you talk about concepts that concern the civilization, not just you:

* What's wrong with capitalism/marxism?
* How much education is enough for modern kids?
* What was the last book you read?
* What is a woman?



<hr/>

The higher you are in the list, the safer is the discussion, and more boring.
A taxi driver and a PhD can be friends if they stay at the top of the list.
Top-level topics produce shallow smalltalk with no meaninful outcomes.
And zero risk.

Lower topic require more intelligence and involve more risk.
They help you discover true similarities and differences between you.
